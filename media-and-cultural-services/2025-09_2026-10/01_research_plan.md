## Case selection

Candidates after discovery: Dresden State Art Collections (January 2026), shared ticketing provider ransomware (March 2026), British Library recovery (attack October 2023), Lee Enterprises (attack February 2025), RTV Noord (November 2025). Dresden was selected as the only recent EU case with a Tier 1 chronology from the institution, its ministry, the prosecutor and the state criminal police. The ticketing case is analysed as a pattern case in Chapters 2, 4 and 6, not as a named incident, because no Tier 1 source records the incident itself (the national library's notice returned a server error). Following the editorial challenge, the live-events group breach notice (Tier 1 regulator register and court complaint, October 2026) was added as named incident 5.

## Evidence handling rules applied

- Tier 3 sources never stand alone for a material finding; Tier 3-only cases are context.
- Leak-site content, stolen or pirated content, hijacked broadcast content and personal data were not accessed or reproduced.
- Hacktivist and threat-actor claims are treated as unconfirmed claims.
- Publication and observation dates are recorded separately; historical observations are labelled.
- Pages blocked by robots rules, proxy policy or server errors were not retrieved through alternative routes; they are recorded as limitations.
