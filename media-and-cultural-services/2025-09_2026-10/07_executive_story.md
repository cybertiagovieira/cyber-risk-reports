Reporting period 1 September 2025 to 15 October 2026. Evidence cut-off 15 October 2026. Seven self-contained analytical slides; no speaker notes. Full sources, dates and locators are in 14_sectorial_report.md; slides carry none by design. This file is regenerated from 08_slide_spec.json.

## Storyline

1. Exposure is concentrated in shared front-of-house platforms and live-output integrity and reaches the financial system mainly through revenue lines, media-group credit and long recovery tails.
2. Five incidents meet the evidence standard; shared platforms, signal paths and catalogues are exposure points alongside institutions' own networks.
3. Public financial evidence is sparse and not additive; quantified losses come through curtailed revenue lines and slow recovery, and in one case became additional debt.
4. Three distinct pathways converge on lost revenue channels and long recovery; they are stress narratives, not forecasts.
5. The security system kept running and museums stayed open while revenue channels recovered last.
6. The sector sits outside horizontal cyber regimes; resilience depends on regulated providers and media and product rules.
7. Six evidence-linked priorities address the pathways without asserting any institution's control gaps.
