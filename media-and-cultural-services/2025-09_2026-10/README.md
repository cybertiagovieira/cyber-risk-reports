# Media & Cultural Services - Cyber Sectorial Analysis

**Edition:** baseline (first edition for this sector). **State:** see 12_run_manifest.json.
**Reporting period:** 1 September 2025 to 15 October 2026. **Evidence cut-off:** 15 October 2026. **Report date:** 15 October 2026.
**Geography:** global, primary focus EU/EEA/UK. **Audience:** 1LoD operational management and Risk Committee.
**Boundary:** public external data only; no internal, client or bank-specific information.
**Case study:** Dresden State Art Collections Cyberattack (selected after discovery).

## Read first

1. `15_sectorial_report.docx` (or `14_sectorial_report.md`) - full standalone report with sources and exact dates.
2. `13_cyber_sectorial_analysis.pptx` - seven-slide analytical readout. Slides carry no sources or dates by design; the report is the auditable companion.

## Principal limitations

No EU or UK entity disclosed a quantified cyber cost for an in-period incident; quantified cases are US and Japanese with earlier attack dates. The March 2026 shared ticketing ransomware is analysed as a pattern case, not a named incident, because no Tier 1 source records it. Open regulatory items: Digital Omnibus effect on AI Act Article 50, AVMSD text, national GDPR Article 85 laws, UK telecoms security scope for broadcast transmission. Benchmarks measure targeting, not impact. No schedule was created or changed by this run.
