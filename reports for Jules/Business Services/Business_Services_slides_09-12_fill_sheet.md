## 3. PROV-04 rationale

PROV-04 = **Client-data protection** (control of client data held in the borrower's own and third-party tools, and breach notification). It has the most direct documented credit link in the findings, backed by Tier 1 records: a £14m UK regulator fine on Capita after a breach reaching 325 pension-scheme clients [S-098] and a $25m notification charge in Conduent's annual report [S-101]; EY's 2026 case shows the same pathway is current [S-119].
Alternatives were not selected: payment-approval controls rest mainly on one case whose operations were unaffected (Arup) [S-106], and help-desk identity checks rest on an advisory pattern whose only sector case link is contested (M&S/TCS, C-20) [S-124, S-110, S-111].
