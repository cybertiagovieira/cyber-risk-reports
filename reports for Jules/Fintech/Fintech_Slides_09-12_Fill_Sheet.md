## 3. PROV-04 rationale

**Selected control:** Payment validation and fraud controls, mirrored as **Payment safeguards** on Slide 12. Credential abuse at Sinqia and payment-interface weaknesses in India connect directly to unauthorised transfers and potential loss absorption (S-027; S-060).
**Credit reason:** Losses, blocked transfers and rail restrictions threaten fee income, liquidity and cash flow more immediately for payment-dependent fintech borrowers than a generic technology-control gap (S-027; S-031).
