# Healthcare - Research Plan and Coverage Map

**Run ID:** 20261014-healthcare-global-eu-uk-2025-09_2026-10
**Reporting period:** 1 September 2025 to 14 October 2026. **Evidence cut-off:** 14 October 2026. **Report date:** 14 October 2026.
**Rolling evidence horizon:** 18 months for patterns, comparisons and public loss cases; older material only as labelled historical or legal context.
**Geography:** Global, primary focus European Union (EU), European Economic Area (EEA) and United Kingdom (UK); North American evidence used for comparison and cross-border dependencies.
**Boundary:** Public external data only. No internal, client, patient or bank-specific information is requested, inferred or used. No leaked datasets, leak-site content or personal health information is accessed or reproduced.
**Edition:** Baseline. No prior Healthcare edition exists in the delivery repository, so changes are described against the period rather than against an earlier report.
**Actual research dates:** 15 October 2026 (all access dates are recorded as the actual retrieval date).

## Decision questions

1. Which Healthcare threat, dependency and supplier-concentration pathways require management attention?
2. Which public developments between September 2025 and October 2026 changed operational, care-continuity, financial and regulatory exposure?
3. Which actions, challenge questions and monitoring indicators should First Line of Defence (1LoD) management and the Risk Committee prioritise?

## Case-study selection

Candidates scored qualitatively on recency, sector relevance, geographic relevance, causal clarity, disclosed operational and patient-service outcome, disclosed financial or regulatory outcome, transferability and evidence quality.

| Candidate | Strengths | Weaknesses | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| Clinical Diagnostics NMDL (Netherlands) | EU; Tier 1 operator and inspectorate evidence; in-period care-continuity effect (screening invitations reduced 50% from 22 December 2025) and regulatory finding (13 May 2026); clear third-party concentration mechanism | Incident in July 2025 precedes the period start; no cost disclosure | Selected |
| Stryker global disruption (March 2026) | Recent; Tier 1 financial and operational disclosure; UK NHS supply response | US-headquartered; patient-service effect described only qualitatively | Used in Top 5, financial chapter and Scenario 3 |
| AZ Monica (Belgium, January 2026) | EU hospital; care diversion and emergency capacity effects | Impact detail available only through Tier 3 media relaying hospital statements | Used in Top 5 with evidence caveat |
| Cegedim Sante MLM (France) | EU clinical software; Tier 1 company statement; ministerial intervention | No operational or financial outcome disclosed | Used in Top 5 and Scenario 1 |

## Stopping rule

Research stopped when each of the seven coverage dimensions had Tier 1 or Tier 2 support or a documented targeted search with no qualifying result. Remaining gaps are recorded in 17_research_coverage.csv and in the report's limitations.
