## What changed in this revision

The first edition used one layout on every slide: a conclusion line, a text table and a three-column band for interpretation, implication and limitation. This revision keeps only three constant anchors: the title, the conclusion line and a one-line limitation at the foot. The body of each slide now uses a different layout suited to its content, with native, editable PowerPoint charts and shape-built diagrams. The colour scheme is unchanged: white background, dark text, emerald 00915E accent, plus tints and greys for context. Findings and the evidence cut-off are unchanged.

## Editorial choices

- The case-study trigger (July 2025) precedes the reporting period; its care-continuity and regulatory consequences fall within it, and slide 5 states this.
- AZ Monica values appear only on the slide 2 timeline, as a light bar labelled media-reported. They are not compared with supplier stock cover because AZ Monica is a provider. Boston Scientific's duration runs to substantial recovery, not full recovery, and the slide 4 subtitle says so.
- Cegedim's detection date is not disclosed beyond "late 2025". The dashed line on slide 2 shows a window ending at the disclosure, its start is labelled approximate in the legend, and the legend also explains the evidence cut-off line.
- Slide 7 places each action against the scenario it mainly addresses. Several actions also address other scenarios, and the matrix does not claim otherwise.
- The roadmap on slide 6 separates in-force, applied or passed-deadline dates from future milestones, pending proposals and the NIS2 court referral.
