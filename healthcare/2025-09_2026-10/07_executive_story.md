## Editorial choices

- The case-study trigger (July 2025) precedes the reporting period; its care-continuity and regulatory consequences fall within it, and this is stated in the report.
- AZ Monica impact detail is labelled media-reported on the slide because no primary hospital statement was retrievable.
- The McKesson distributor incident is named in the report but excluded from the Top 5 because scope and materiality were undetermined at filing.
- Benchmarks (survey recovery cost) appear only with an explicit statement that they are not entity estimates.
- After the editorial challenge, judgement 1 was reframed as the most publicly evidenced channel (confidence medium), judgement 4 no longer calls the Stryker event destructive, Cegedim counts are stated as doctor users, pre-period comparators are labelled, slide 5 states that the case trigger precedes the period, and acronyms are expanded on the slides where used.
