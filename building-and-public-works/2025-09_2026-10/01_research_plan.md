# Research Plan - Building & Public Works Cyber Sectorial Analysis

Run ID: 20261014-building-public-works-global-eu-uk-2025-09_2026-10
Reporting period: 1 September 2025 to 14 October 2026. Evidence cut-off: 14 October 2026. Report date: 14 October 2026.
Rolling evidence horizon: 18 months (patterns, comparisons and public loss cases). Older material is labelled contextual or historical.
Boundary: public external data only. No internal, client or bank-specific information is requested, inferred or used.

## Decision questions

1. Which Building & Public Works threat, incident and dependency pathways require management attention in the EU, EEA and UK, with global comparison?
2. Which public developments in the period changed operational, financial or regulatory exposure, and what public outcomes are disclosed?
3. Which actions, challenge questions and monitoring triggers should 1LoD operational management and the Risk Committee prioritise?

## Discovery modules executed

- Construction and civil-engineering incidents (English, German, French, Danish and Japanese queries).
- Infrastructure construction programmes (Fehmarnbelt fixed link).
- Public-sector project owners (London tri-borough shared IT; Swedish municipal supplier).
- Public-works operational technology (Minnesota water systems; Bremanger dam, contextual).
- Building materials and equipment (Dragos supplier statement; SANKO TECHNO).
- Construction technology platforms and cloud fourth parties (Procore 10-K; AWS Middle East impairment, secondary only).
- Building-automation systems (CRA scope; no in-period Tier 1/2 incident found).
- Payment-chain fraud (AFP construction BEC alert).
- Regulation (NIS2, CER, CRA, GDPR, EU procurement proposal, UK NIS, UK bill, PPN 014, Procurement Act 2023, DPA 2018, SEC comparative).

## Case selection (after discovery)

Candidates scored on recency, sector relevance, geography, causal clarity, disclosed outcome, transferability and evidence quality: Femern Link Contractors (October 2026), Kensington and Chelsea shared-IT attack (November 2025), Miljödata (August 2025 attack, September 2026 fine), Minnesota water OT (July 2026), SANKO TECHNO (August 2026). Selected: Kensington and Chelsea Shared-IT Cyber Attack, because it is the only in-period case with Tier 1 chronology, quantified cost, auditor findings and service-level restoration dates. Femern is retained as the leading monitoring item for the next edition.

## Exclusions

- Leak-site victim claims without Tier 1 or Tier 2 corroboration are not used as named incidents.
- Secondary-only figures (for example cross-sector economic-loss estimates relayed by media) are excluded from material findings.
- No numerical probabilities are assigned to scenarios.
