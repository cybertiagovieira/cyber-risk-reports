# Building & Public Works - Cyber Sectorial Analysis

**Edition:** baseline (first edition for this sector). **State:** see 12_run_manifest.json.
**Reporting period:** 1 September 2025 to 14 October 2026. **Evidence cut-off:** 14 October 2026. **Report date:** 14 October 2026.
**Geography:** global, primary focus EU/EEA/UK. **Audience:** 1LoD operational management and Risk Committee.
**Boundary:** public external data only; no internal, client or bank-specific information.
**Case study:** Kensington and Chelsea Shared-IT Cyber Attack (selected after discovery).

## Read first

1. `15_sectorial_report.docx` (or `14_sectorial_report.md`) - full standalone report with sources and exact dates.
2. `13_cyber_sectorial_analysis.pptx` - seven-slide analytical readout. Slides carry no sources or dates by design; the report is the auditable companion.

## Principal limitations

No contractor or supplier disclosed a quantified cyber cost, delay or contractual effect in the period; public-owner cases are over-represented; Fehmarnbelt effects are media-reported and unconfirmed; leak-site counts measure claims, not attack rates. No schedule was created or changed by this run.
