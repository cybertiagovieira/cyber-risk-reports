# Manufacturing - Cyber Sectorial Analysis

**Edition:** baseline (first edition for this sector). **State:** see 12_run_manifest.json.
**Reporting period:** 1 September 2025 to 16 October 2026. **Evidence cut-off:** 16 October 2026. **Report date:** 16 October 2026.
**Geography:** global, primary focus EU/EEA/UK. **Audience:** 1LoD operational management and Risk Committee.
**Boundary:** public external data only; no internal, client or bank-specific information.
**Case study:** Jaguar Land Rover Cyber Incident (selected after discovery).

## Read first

1. `15_sectorial_report.docx` (or `14_sectorial_report.md`) - full standalone report with sources and exact dates.
2. `13_cyber_sectorial_analysis.pptx` - seven-slide analytical readout. Slides carry no sources or dates by design; the report is the auditable companion.

## Principal limitations

Quantified outcomes come from listed companies (JLR, Asahi, US medical-device makers); no EU or UK small supplier disclosed losses, no ransom or insurance recovery was disclosed, and no official source attributes a supplier insolvency to a cyber incident. The JLR economy-wide loss is modelled. The French refrigeration-controller case was days old at the cut-off. Several EUR-Lex and CISA pages did not render in retrieval and are flagged in the registers. Regimes listed as not assessed in the report carry no findings. No schedule was created or changed by this run.
