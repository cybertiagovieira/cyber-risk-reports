# Manufacturing - Research Plan and Coverage Map

**Run:** 20261016-manufacturing-global-eu-uk-2025-09_2026-10. **Reporting period:** 1 September 2025 to 16 October 2026. **Evidence cut-off:** 16 October 2026. **Rolling evidence horizon:** 18 months, with older material labelled contextual or historical. **Boundary:** public external data only. **Edition:** baseline; no prior report for this sector.

## Decision questions

1. Which Manufacturing threat, incident, production-disruption and supplier-cascade pathways require management attention in the EU, EEA and UK, with global comparison?
2. Which public developments in the period changed production, delivery, supply-chain, financial, macro-economic or regulatory exposure, and what outcomes are disclosed?
3. Which actions, challenge questions and monitoring triggers should 1LoD operational management and the Risk Committee prioritise?

## Case selection

Candidates after discovery: Jaguar Land Rover (September 2025), Asahi Group (September 2025), Stryker (March 2026), Boston Scientific (August 2026), West Pharmaceutical Services (May 2026) and the French refrigeration-controller compromise (October 2026). Jaguar Land Rover was selected as the only in-period case with company filings over four quarters, a statistics-office and central-bank record of output effects, a government loan guarantee, a supplier-financing response and an independent loss model, in the UK.

## Evidence handling rules applied

- Tier 3 sources never stand alone for a material finding; Tier 3-only cases are context.
- Leak-site content, hacktivist channels, stolen data, exploit code and personal data were not accessed or reproduced.
- Threat-actor and hacktivist claims are treated as unconfirmed claims; no state attribution is made without official attribution.
- Production stoppages are classified by mechanism: precautionary IT shutdown, IT dependency failure or direct OT compromise.
- Publication and observation dates are recorded separately; historical observations are labelled.
- Pages blocked or not rendered by the retrieval tool were not obtained by other routes; they are recorded as limitations.
- No schedule was created or modified.
